Privacy

Updated October 20, 2026

Browsing this website

This informational website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its current pages contain no inquiry form, account registration, payment entry, first-party analytics code, or browser-storage code. Page scripts animate content as you scroll. The site's logo and stylesheet are served with the site; the inspected pages do not load third-party media or font services.

Your browser and the hosting provider still process connection information, which can include IP address, requested pages and technical details. This is not a promise that browsing creates no logs or cookies. See GitHub's privacy statement for the host's practices.

Phone, text and email

Contact links open your calling, messaging or email provider. If you contact True Owner Recovery, the information you choose to provide, such as your name, contact details and claim reference, is used to respond to your inquiry. Those channels and their providers have their own processing practices. Do not send Social Security numbers, bank credentials or sensitive identity documents in an initial message.

Handling questions and requests

This notice does not set a fixed retention period or promise deletion, confidentiality, non-sharing, or a particular security outcome. Ask us about the handling, recipients and retention of your specific correspondence before sending sensitive material. For privacy questions or an accessibility barrier, email trueownerrecovery@gmail.com or use the contact options. Describe the page and assistance needed; do not include sensitive claim records.
