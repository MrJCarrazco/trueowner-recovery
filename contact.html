What happens when you contact us

  1. We confirm we're talking to the right person. A couple of basic questions — never your Social Security number, bank details, or any payment information.
  2. We explain what we found. Which office holds the funds, which former property they're connected to, and how to verify it yourself.
  3. You decide — on your schedule. If you want our help, we send a plain-language agreement. If you'd rather claim on your own, we'll part as friends: you may be able to claim these funds yourself without paying anyone — our service is optional.
